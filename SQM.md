## I. Comparison of ISO/IEC 25010, ISO 9001 and ISO/IEC 12207

| Standard          | Purpose & Scope                                                                            | Contribution to Software Quality                                                                                                     |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **ISO/IEC 25010** | Defines a **software product quality model**.                                              | Helps evaluate qualities such as **performance, security, reliability, maintainability, usability, and functionality**.              |
| **ISO 9001**      | Provides a **Quality Management System (QMS)** for organizations.                          | Ensures systematic quality management through **documentation, monitoring, audits, corrective actions, and continuous improvement**. |
| **ISO/IEC 12207** | Defines **software life-cycle processes** from requirements to maintenance and retirement. | Provides a structured and consistent approach for **developing, operating, and maintaining software**.                               |

### How They Complement Each Other

The three standards focus on different aspects of quality:

* **ISO/IEC 12207** → defines **how software development processes should be carried out**.
* **ISO 9001** → defines **how quality should be managed within the organization**.
* **ISO/IEC 25010** → defines **what quality characteristics the software should achieve**.

Thus, together they provide a complete approach to software quality throughout the **software development life cycle**.

---

## II. Application to the Banking Application

All three standards can be applied because the company has both **software quality problems and process problems**.

### 1. Performance, Security, Maintainability and Reliability

**Use: ISO/IEC 25010**

It can be used to define and evaluate the required quality characteristics:

* **Performance:** improve response time and resource usage.
* **Security:** strengthen authentication, authorization, and data protection.
* **Maintainability:** improve code structure and ease of modification.
* **Reliability:** improve availability, fault tolerance, and recovery.

### 2. Lack of Systematic Quality Management

**Use: ISO 9001**

ISO 9001 can establish a **Quality Management System** by defining quality policies, documentation, monitoring, audits, corrective actions, and continuous improvement.

### 3. Lack of Defined Life-Cycle Processes

**Use: ISO/IEC 12207**

ISO/IEC 12207 can provide structured processes for **requirements, design, development, testing, deployment, operation, and maintenance**.

### Conclusion

**ISO/IEC 12207** provides the **development process**, **ISO 9001** provides **quality management**, and **ISO/IEC 25010** provides the **software quality characteristics**. Using all three together can make the banking application more **secure, reliable, efficient, and maintainable** while improving the organization's overall development process.
